01 — THE BEGINNING

And then there was us.

There wasn't some dramatic announcement that our story had begun. It was simply a collection of moments that kept making me want one more conversation, one more laugh, one more memory with you.

“Somehow, ordinary days started feeling special when you were in them.”
02 — THE CUTE PART

The kind of cute only we understand.

Our stupid jokes, our random expressions, the way we can turn absolutely nothing into something worth remembering. We are, honestly, two same-same idiots—and I wouldn't change that.

“Same brain. Same stupidity. Somehow, my favourite combination.”
03 — THAT MOMENT

The kiss I'll always remember.

Some moments don't need a long explanation. They just stay. That little moment between us felt like the world had gone quiet for a second—and I was exactly where I wanted to be.

“One tiny moment. A thousand feelings.”
04 — THE HUG

Your hugs feel like home.

There are hugs that are just hugs, and then there are the ones where you don't want to let go. Yours somehow make everything feel a little quieter, softer, and better.

“If I could pause one feeling, it would probably be this one.”
05 — OUR CHAOS

We are ridiculously unserious.

And honestly? Good. Life already takes itself too seriously. I'm glad I have someone with whom I can be completely stupid, laugh at the dumbest things and create memories that make no sense to anyone else.

“Normal is overrated. Give me our chaos.”
06 — MY FAVOURITE VIEW

Still can't believe you're mine to love.

Sometimes I look at your photos and think about how lucky I am to know the person behind that smile. You're beautiful, yes—but it's the little things about you that stay with me the most.

“Pretty face, prettier heart. And yes, I had to say it.”
07 — OUTSIDE OUR LITTLE WORLD

Everywhere feels better with you.

Whether it was a proper plan or one of those random moments that somehow became a memory, I love how easily you make a place feel familiar.

08 — THE REAL YOU

The candid moments are my favourites.

Not the perfectly posed ones. The laughing ones. The distracted ones. The ones where you're simply being you. Those are the moments I want to remember forever.

09 — US

My favourite picture has two people in it.

Because when I see us together, I don't just see a photograph. I see everything behind it—the conversations, the laughs, the little fights, the making-up, the hugs, and the love.

“This isn't just a photo. It's a tiny piece of our story.”
10 — MY FAVOURITE CHAPTER

And I hope this isn't the last page.

Ten photos are nowhere near enough. There are too many moments I want to keep, too many places I want to see with you, and way too many memories still waiting for us.
